ABOUT

Montreal-based DJ Cat Glam brings an eclectic musical universe where cultures and diversity meet. Her sets blend global, tropical and urban sounds, mixing Afrobeats, Dancehall, Hip-Hop, classics and more into a vibrant, unifying musical experience.

Behind the decks since 2022, Cat’s passion for music dates back to her childhood, surrounded by piano melodies and the rhythms of percussion. Always drawn to music in all its forms, she naturally found her way to DJing.

A graphic designer and visual artist, Cat also moves within the creative world. This artistic sensibility shapes her musical approach, while allowing her selections to speak through their own language.
